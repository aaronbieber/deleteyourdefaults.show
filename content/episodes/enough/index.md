What if you didn't have to earn your enoughness?

In this episode, Rosa and Aaron explore the ways a sense of "not enough" can
drive overwork, anxious attachment, perfectionism, blame, and the constant
search for external validation. We unpack the difference between fault and
responsibility, why discomfort often appears when you begin changing old
patterns, and how awareness creates the possibility of choice.

We also offer a gentler path: noticing moments of enoughness, letting them land
in the body, releasing the belief that being hard on yourself will make you
better, and asking a deceptively powerful question: What if this could be
easier---or even more fun?
