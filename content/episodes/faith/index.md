What is faith when you let go of religion, certainty, and the idea that
everything is going according to a predetermined plan?

In this episode, Rosa and Aaron explore faith as trust in yourself, in
life, and in the adventure of not knowing. We talk about friendly versus
neutral universes, the power of mantras, why certainty might ruin the
game, and how "losing is part of winning."

Along the way, we consider what changes when we treat life as an
infinite game---one where the point isn't to defeat everyone else, but
to keep playing, enjoy the experience, and create more goodness as we
go.
