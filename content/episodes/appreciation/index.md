You wrote down three things you're grateful for. But did you actually feel
anything?

In this episode, we explore appreciation as a choice, why a gratitude practice
can turn into another chore, and what changes when you let it touch your heart.
Along the way: differing tastes in bacon, the desire hiding inside "I have to,"
and a brief pause to practice being "amorphously grateful" together.

We also explore making room for grief, shame, and resistance---and finding
something to appreciate even there. Plus, a reminder: your goodness isn't
something you have to earn.
