Ever wanted to be happy for someone and felt a sharp stab of jealousy
instead? When a mentor landed a dream speaking gig, Rosa felt both.
Getting curious about that sting helped her hear what was underneath
it. Find out in this episode\!

Rosa Carson and Aaron Bieber get into envy, jealousy, and their close
friend: resentment. Aaron shares how getting curious about jealousy in
his relationships helped him notice the fear underneath it. Rosa
explores what happens when you let yourself feel the sting in your
body and make room for gratitude alongside it. We also locate the
exact point where woo becomes woof. (Okay, we debate it.)
