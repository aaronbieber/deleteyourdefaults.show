In this episode, we explore forgiveness as a practice of freedom: letting go of
resentment, taking responsibility for what's actually yours, and loosening the
stories of blame and judgment that keep us stuck.

We talk about forgiving other people, forgiving past versions of ourselves, why
responsibility can actually feel *good*, and how forgiveness makes room for
self-trust, possibility, and a whole lot more life.

Delete Your Defaults is our show about hacking your social autopilot. If
