What does it even mean to be "the best" when life is multidimensional,
subjective, and constantly changing? In this episode, we unpack the ways our
obsession with bestness feeds perfectionism and self-criticism---and how
redefining "best" for yourself can make room for more play, resilience,
connection, and actual enjoyment.

Along the way: climbing, yoga, Michelin stars, Conan the Barbarian,
failure as a practice, and the possibility that even an experience you
absolutely did not want may contain something worth finding.
